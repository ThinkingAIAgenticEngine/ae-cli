# Project semantic knowledge base compile rules

These rules guide Schema generation for project semantic knowledge bases built from source tree ZIP uploads.

- Generate durable rules. Never copy working directories, absolute or temporary paths, source IDs, run IDs, or machine paths. Refer to "the ZIP source tree" and use stable `sources/...` paths only when needed. README files are filing guides, not business evidence.
- `sources/project/pskb-p*-refresh-state.md` and its numbered `-part-*.md` files are CLI machine state. Do not read, decode, cite, or compile them as business evidence; use the authored project and asset sources instead.
- CLI-managed asset facts live under `sources/assets/**`. Adopted personal semantics exported by the asset package live under `sources/personal-semantics/**` and are also CLI-managed generated source. Human project background, business rules, meetings, decisions, corrections, and references live outside those generated directories; asset refresh must not delete or override those human inputs.
- Organize generated Wiki pages by scope and business use, not by mirroring every source file. The primary navigation has five ordered entries: project overview, project-wide shared knowledge, business domains, event and asset catalog, and governance with provenance. Within each domain, explain the business question, judgment path, relevant events, metrics, assets, and boundaries. Keep asset-type pages as secondary evidence and lookup indexes.
- Begin the root page with question-intent routing. Route business-state and current-value questions through the matching domain and recall card; route named assets and field/definition/dependency questions through the asset catalog and governance; route shared product background through project-wide knowledge. If no card matches, enter the relevant domain and filter assets by business object, answer grain, filters and time. If no suitable evidence remains, state the gap. Stop searching once the smallest authoritative evidence set matches object, grain, scope and time.
- Each recall card must identify its triggering questions, business object and answer grain, required parameters, conditional branches with direct asset targets, excluded or non-interchangeable assets, a stop condition, and a fallback for unavailable, conflicting or unmatched evidence. Preserve these as instructions in the compiled card; a fixed preferred-then-fallback sequence is insufficient when branches use different definitions.
- Public event groups are a project event lookup taxonomy. A group such as a product module is not automatically a business domain or an event definition. Only include events present in the exported asset scope, preserve their event-name identity, and link to the canonical event page. Never infer business meaning, certification, or domain membership from a group label alone. Personal event catalogs must not enter a shared project Wiki.
- The project overview must start with the project-level business model: business positioning, audience roles, business objects, object relationships, decision chains, asset roles in those chains, and non-goals. Asset counts, snapshot hashes, source package metadata and build logs belong after that model. Do not reduce the overview to a directory, asset inventory, or build summary.
- Do not infer business meaning from titles alone. Preserve unknowns, conflicts, source scope, certification state, and provenance when evidence is incomplete.
- Distinguish query counts, distinct report counts, company counts and people counts; never substitute one grain for another from similarly named fields. A scoped personal-semantic method for customer report usage belongs only on its verified scenario card and must link the actual search event, company field, report-name field, report asset and adoption record. Mark unverified field equivalence or name uniqueness as unknown.
- If the governed package contains neither certified entry assets nor adopted personal semantics, compile the uploaded empty skeleton as an explicit unknown-state Wiki with the normal five navigation layers. Do not invent business domains, decisions, metrics, or asset definitions. When later evidence replaces the skeleton, remove obsolete empty-state claims from the published Wiki.
- Corrections and decisions override other material only when they state target, content, date, status, and applicable scope.


## Navigation and page naming

The top-level `index.md` is decision-oriented. Keep its five primary entries; do not add a sitemap, individual assets or properties, numeric IDs, containers, or raw filenames. Put recall cards under domains, event groups under the event map, asset indexes under the catalog, and individual assets in category/domain/container pages. A full sitemap belongs in `wiki/index.md` or category indexes, never the root.

Both indexes use these primary targets: `wiki/project-overview.md`, `wiki/common/index.md`, `wiki/domains/index.md`, `wiki/asset-index/index.md`, `wiki/governance/index.md`. Put `wiki/event-map/index.md` below the catalog; keep asset, card, and adoption pages reachable below the five entries.

Adopted personal semantics are source provenance, not a top-level knowledge category. Integrate each accepted conclusion into its declared project, domain, recall-card, or asset target with its applicable boundary; retain a knowledge-adoption record under governance. A personal preference remains scoped and must not be turned into a project-wide rule merely because it was adopted. Use the label "project supplementary knowledge" for the integrated content and "knowledge adoption record" for its provenance.

Navigation taxonomy version 2.1 adds root intent routing and conditional recall-card execution. On a refresh from an older version, perform a full Wiki rebuild from current sources and regenerate the root index and cards; do not reuse obsolete published pages or retain an old top-level personal-semantic page. Preserve human source files and current canonical asset facts. After publication, verify routing, no-card fallback, card branches, stop conditions and all five navigation entries. A valid file count alone does not prove answer quality.

When linking to an individual asset, use its business title as the visible link text and keep the numeric ID only as supporting identity beside the title, for example `示例报表（report 1001）`. Do not render bare numeric links such as `1001` as navigation items.

## Precompiled source contract

CLI Agent has already performed the main semantic compilation for ALL assets: business meanings, SQL interpretation, calculation definitions, relationships, domains, recall boundaries, and conflict findings. These are editable source documents, not an imported final Wiki snapshot. Reuse their complete prose and calculation facts. Do not repeat project-wide extraction, SQL parsing, semantic planning, or per-field rewriting.

Consume adopted personal semantics by type: `background` informs context, `preference` informs routing and style, `experience` supplies proven cautions, and `asset_context` annotates an existing referenced asset. Retain person name, source/update time, source IDs, and scope; never expose `open_id`. Fuse similar conclusions without widening scope. They cannot override governed definitions, SQL facts, corrections, decisions, or live results.

Business-domain pages need a judgment model and `Agent 使用摘要` for first-hop routing: business state, objects, signals, decision path, primary/supporting/drilldown/excluded assets, events, metrics, and dimensions. Do not replace this with a report list or regenerate it without newer scoped evidence.

Reject or repair any generated Wiki shape where the root, overview, or business-domain pages only list questions, events, metrics, dimensions, reports, dashboards or counts without explaining the business judgment chain that makes those assets useful. Examples in source instructions are illustrative only; never hardcode project-specific business domains, assets, metrics, event names or decision chains into the compiled Wiki.

Compile and publish the Wiki with prepared asset sections, linked references, concise titled navigation, and scoped human context. Batch independent writes and link checks; reuse unchanged pages.

For SQL assets, keep Agent-authored purpose, parameters, output fields, grain, filters, limits, applicable/non-applicable questions, and evidence locator in one `Agent 使用摘要`. Raw SQL/index JSON stays local; do not infer missing details. Keep unknowns, certification, dependency-only status, conflicts, and blocked recall; do not dismiss safety states as stale placeholders. Never expose `CLI Agent`, `摘要来源`, `semantic_plan`, parser fallback, or builder/debug terms in Wiki prose.
- `dependency_only` is an answer-authority limit, not a blanket ban on retrieval. Known metadata may support definition, dependency or conflict explanations. It cannot independently prove business state or live numeric results. If platform permissions block direct metadata retrieval, follow its authorized referencing asset and say what remains unverified.

For non-SQL reports, keep `Agent 使用摘要` useful for business routing, not a mechanical parser echo. Purpose and applicable questions must differ. Preserve evidenced metrics, dimensions, and filters; where unavailable, direct the reader to confirm in the source report instead of using broad `未识别/未发现` placeholders.

During incremental compilation, update changed sources and dependent pages, indexes, and cross-source conclusions; reuse unrelated pages and remove obsolete assertions. Adopted personal semantics share generated sources' paths, hashes, and add/replace/remove lifecycle. Integrate human evidence by scope, status, and authority rather than recency; distinguish scope differences from contradictions and preserve uploaded-source provenance.
